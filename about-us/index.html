    2023: The Idea

    It all started when a friend who had a travel agency approached us with an idea to create a chat bot that would help them optimize managers' workload and streamline leads qualification. Having seen how that single automation changed their ROMI we saw a great potential in a service that could enable businesses use automated workflows to save a great deal of time on routine operations.

    2024: Launch

    Further on our CTO Boris Sotsky created a state machine that efficiently supports complex workflows and lets businesses quickly add new integrations to cater custom needs. That's how AddProcess was born with an initial focus on travel agencies where growing inquiry volumes and manual communication were creating bottlenecks for sales teams.

    2026: Scaling

    As we grew, we expanded beyond travel into real estate, insurance, home services, and other service-based industries. We introduced customizable AI agents, CRM integrations, and automated workflows evolving into a powerful no-code platform. users can now leverage a visual builder, ready-made templates, with an MCP enabled automations building.

    What We Believe

    We empower companies to build connected, efficient and user-centric leadgen and support solutions. Leveraging the power of AI agents and scripted workflows helps to make the most from website traffic and handle repetitive inquiries without burnouts.

    Having started in 2024 we have helped 50+ companies across travel, real estate, home services, etc. optimize their process of leads qualification and accelerate growth.

    Headquartered in Warsaw, serving founders across the EU & UK.
