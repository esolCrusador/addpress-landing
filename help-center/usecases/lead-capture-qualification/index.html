    Lead capture & qualification

    Qualify inbound leads in chat and hand them to sales with the conversation and answers stored.

    Bot greets on Telegram, Instagram, or Web Chat, asks qualifying questions, stores answers (e.g. Sheets or AmoCRM), and lets sales follow up via chat history or the CRM.

    Who it is for

    Sales and marketing teams who want inbound leads qualified in chat before a human steps in.

    Desired outcome

    1. Prospect starts a chat on a brand channel.
    2. Bot welcomes them and walks through qualifying questions.
    3. Answers are stored in a spreadsheet or CRM.
    4. Staff review the conversation (and CRM/sheet) and follow up.

    How (features)

    • Connect a channel bot
    • Activate a bot
    • Welcome experience and/or Bot menu navigation
    • In-chat survey or Build a conversational scenario
    • Integration from a flow (Google Sheets / AmoCRM)
    • Browse chat history

    Optional: Slash commands / Keyword & attachment triggers as alternate entry points.

    Product areas involved

    • Bots
    • Bot assignments
    • Menus (optional entry)
    • Scenarios, Surveys
    • Integrations, Google Sheets / AmoCRM
    • Chat history

    Caveats

    • The Portal Leads page is mock/demo UI today. Do not rely on it for production follow-up; use chat history and/or CRM/Sheets integrations instead. See Lead tracking.
    • Welcome dialog UI is currently enabled for Web Chat assignments; on Telegram/Instagram use commands, triggers, or the first scenario/menu step as the greeting.
