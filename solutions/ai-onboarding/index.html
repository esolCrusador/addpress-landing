    An AI Agent That Helps Customers Get Things Done

    From customer onboarding to website workflow automation, AddProcess helps your AI agent do more than answer questions — it helps customers get things done.

    Guide customers through onboarding

    The AI agent asks relevant questions, explains next steps, collects required information, and adapts the conversation to the customer's needs.

    Connect conversations to business tools

    Use MCP (Model Context Protocol) to connect the AI agent to supported business systems, allowing it to retrieve relevant information or execute supported actions within configured permissions.

    Automate website workflows

    Turn repetitive website interactions into conversational workflows — from lead qualification and information collection to supported service requests and account setup processes.
