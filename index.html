    Everything You Need to Convert More Leads

    AddProcess combines AI intelligence with scripted workflow automations to transform how you handle incoming leads.

    AI-Powered Qualification

    RAG "Brain" enables chat bot seamlessly qualify leads using data it can scrape from your website and knowledge base you provide.

    Real-Time CRM Sync

    The AI naturally extracts key data points during the conversation (Name, Phone, Care Needs, Timeline, Budget) creating a structured "Lead Profile" and syncing it with your CRM.

    Automated Notification System

    The system auto-emails the contact immediately when a lead is generated. The email includes the Lead Qualification Profile summary and a direct link to the full transcript in the dashboard.

    Drag-and-Drop Conversation Flows

    Build rich agent scenarios with a visual builder and a library of action blocks — messaging, surveys, logic, and integrations — without writing code.

    Multi-Channel Publishing

    Launch the same agent across your website, WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, SMS, or APIs so leads are captured wherever they message you.

    Full Chat Transcripts

    Every conversation is saved in your leads dashboard with a complete transcript, so your team can follow up with full context and close faster.
