    How credits work

    1. 1

      A credit is spent on each agent reply.

      Your customers' messages are free. The greeting, your team's replies and training the agent on your website and files don't use credits.

    2. 2

      The cost of a reply depends on the AI model.

      The most economical model costs 1 credit per reply; more powerful ones cost more. A ready answer you wrote yourself costs 1 credit. For estimates we count 3 credits per conversation, which is where the conversation numbers in the table come from.

    3. 3

      Plan credits renew every month.

      On your subscription date. Unused plan credits don't roll over.

    4. 4

      Need more? Top up.

      On paid plans you can buy credit packs from 200 to 10,000 as a one-off payment. A pack of 200 credits starts at $11. Top-up credits never expire and are used after your monthly allowance.
